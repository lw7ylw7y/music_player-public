Music Player: UX mockups

These are fixtures, not screenshots of a built app. Hand-generated HTML with invented songs and artists. They show layout, wording and states from docs/UX Design.md. They were not rendered on an Android phone or emulator, so real fonts, system bars, the keyboard, Android Auto and the widget picker will differ.

Icons are Material-style drawings for illustration. Sizes: phone frames are 360 × 720 dp. Light and dark follow the phone's setting; every control has a word or label, not only a color.
